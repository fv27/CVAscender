# CVAscender — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

CVAscender helps high-school students in Switzerland find opportunities (olympiads, competitions, clubs, camps, volunteering and more) and build their CV.

**Everything stays on your device.** Your answers (first name if you give one, canton, town, age, school year, interests, skills), your saved, applied and completed listings, and the activities you add are stored only on your iPhone, in a file encrypted by iOS. We have no server for the app and cannot see this data.

**No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

**Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

**Images:** the app loads illustrative photos from Unsplash and organisers' logos from public icon services (Google). These services receive your device's IP address when an image loads, like any website, but nothing about you or your answers.

**Links** to official websites open in Safari; those websites have their own privacy policies.

**Deleting your data:** Settings → "Reset everything and start again", or delete the app.

**Children and young people:** CVAscender is made for students aged about 12–19 and works without any account.

**Your rights:** under Swiss data protection law (and the GDPR where it applies) you can ask us about your data. For the app, all data is on your device and you can delete it yourself.
